Privacy Policy for HypHex: World Conquest

Effective Date: April 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the "HypHex: World Conquest" mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect
When you register and play the App, we collect the following categories of information:

Authentication Data (Third-Party Logins): If you log in using Apple or Google, we collect your name, email address, and a unique user identifier (UID) provided by these services. We do NOT store or have access to your third-party account passwords.

Game & Profile Data: We store your in-game progress, territorial conquest data (hexagonal grid coordinates), virtual currency (Hex Coin) balances, gamer tags, and referral relationships (who invited whom).

Device & Advertising Data: We use mobile advertising identifiers (such as Apple’s IDFA or Google’s Advertising ID) to serve and personalize advertisements within the App via Google AdMob.

2. How We Use Your Information
Your data is used strictly for:

Providing secure authentication and account management.

Synchronizing game progress and world conquest data across devices.

Processing referral rewards and maintaining the game’s economy.

Displaying advertisements to support the free-to-play model of the App.

3. Third-Party Data Processors
We share data with the following trusted providers to ensure the App functions correctly:

Supabase: Our primary backend and database provider. Your data is stored securely on their cloud infrastructure.

Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device-specific information to serve relevant ads. You can manage ad tracking in your device settings.

Apple & Google OAuth: Used solely for secure sign-in and identity verification.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion (Mandatory Disclosure)
We believe in your right to privacy and data ownership.

Right to Delete: You have the right to delete your account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Exercise This Right: You can initiate a permanent deletion directly within the App by navigating to Profile -> Settings -> Delete Account.

Effect of Deletion: Once confirmed, our system triggers a cascade deletion that permanently removes your authentication records, email address, profile information, and all game-related progress from our active databases.

5. International Data Rights (GDPR & KVKK)
Users in the European Union (GDPR) and Turkey (KVKK) have specific rights regarding their personal data, including the right to access, correct, or erase their information. We honor these rights globally for all our players.

6. Children's Privacy
HypHex: World Conquest does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately for removal.
