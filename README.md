Privacy Policy for HypHex: World Conquest
Effective Date: April 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the “HypHex: World Conquest” mobile application (the “App”).

1. Information We Collect
When you register and play the App, we collect the following categories of information:

Authentication Data: If you log in using Apple or Google, we collect your name, email address, and a unique user identifier (UID). We do NOT store or have access to your passwords.

Game, Profile & Leaderboard Data: We store your in-game progress, manually selected territorial coordinates (hexagonal grid), virtual currency (Hex Coin) balances, gamer tags, referral data, and game scores. Please note: By creating an account and playing the game, you explicitly consent to your gamer tag, game scores, and conquered territory statistics being uploaded to our servers. This data is collected to display your ranking publicly on our global leaderboard.

Location Transparency: The App does NOT track or collect your precise GPS location. Users manually interact with a map to select regions. We only store the coordinates of the areas you choose to interact with as part of the game mechanics.

Device & Advertising Data: We use mobile advertising identifiers (Apple’s IDFA or Google’s Advertising ID) to serve advertisements via Google AdMob.

2. How We Use Your Information
Your data is used strictly for:

Providing secure authentication and account management.

Operating the global leaderboard, which includes publicly displaying your gamer tag and game scores to other players worldwide to enable competitive gameplay.

Synchronizing game progress and manually selected conquest data across devices.

Processing referral rewards and maintaining the game’s economy.

Displaying advertisements to support the App’s free-to-play model.

3. Third-Party Data Processors
We share data with the following trusted providers:

Supabase: Our primary backend provider. Your data (excluding GPS location) is stored securely on their infrastructure.

Google AdMob: Displays ads. AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID to show relevant ads. You can disable this in your device settings.

Apple & Google OAuth: Used solely for secure identity verification.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion
We believe in your right to privacy and data ownership.

Right to Delete: You have the right to delete your account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Exercise This Right: You can initiate permanent deletion within the App via Profile -> Settings -> Delete Account.

Effect of Deletion: Once confirmed, all your data (email, profile, and progress) is immediately and permanently removed from our active databases, including the global leaderboard.

5. International Data Rights (GDPR & KVKK)
Users in the European Union (GDPR) and Turkey (KVKK) have specific rights regarding their personal data. We honor these rights (access, correction, erasure) globally for all our players.

6. Children’s Privacy
HypHex does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.
